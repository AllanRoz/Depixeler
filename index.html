OBJECTIVE
You are excavating ancient colossal pixel monoliths. Embedded deep inside each formation lies a rare ✦ Golden Core. Destroy it to complete the sector!

CONTROLS
🖱 Aim & Launch: Drag or click on the arena to aim the trajectory beam and release to fire.
🖱 Pan & Zoom: Scroll wheel or pinch to zoom. Middle-drag, right-drag, or WASD to pan.
⚡ Quick Recall: Hit the RECALL button to instantly return flying orbs.
⏩ Fast Forward: Toggle 1X, 2X, or 3X game simulation speed.

ORB TYPES
⚪ Kinetic: Reliable, heavy damage, emits kinetic shockwaves.
⚡ Lightning: Zaps nearby pixels with electric arcs on every strike.
☠ Poison: Corrodes pixels with spreading acidic slime.
🔴 Laser: Pierces straight through 1-HP pixels without stopping.

🔵 Normal (1HP) • 🟣 Hard (3HP) • 🔷 Armored (8HP)
